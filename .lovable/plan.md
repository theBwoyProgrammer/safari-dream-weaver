# Tembo Safari Lodge Rebrand

## Goal
Transform the existing tour-company website into a lodge website for Tembo Safari Lodge in Katunguru, Queen Elizabeth National Park.

## What will change
- Replace Rio Safaris branding throughout with Tembo Safari Lodge and the tagline “Feel the culture, love the stay.”
- Rework the home page around the lodge, Kazinga Channel setting, accommodation, dining, and nearby activities.
- Turn the Tours page into an Accommodation page with the supplied room categories and rates:
  - Standard single: FB $90, HB $80, BB $70
  - Double/twin: FB $120, HB $100, BB $90
- Update the About page with the supplied location, travel times, cottages, room features, views, restaurant, and bar.
- Adapt Gallery and Blog wording to lodge stays, Queen Elizabeth National Park, dining, and guest activities.
- Update all calls to action and contact sections with:
  - +256 701 628 803
  - +256 772 423 037
  - info@tembosafari.com
  - P.O. Box 23008, Queen Elizabeth National Park, Katunguru
  - Open daily for travellers
- Update navigation labels, footer links, page metadata, and the location map for Katunguru.
- Use the supplied flyer as authentic Tembo imagery while retaining suitable wildlife photography already on the site.

## Interaction
- Keep the existing enquiry form, changing its choices to room, dining, group visit, directions, and activities.
- Make phone numbers and email addresses directly clickable.
- Preserve mobile-friendly navigation, image gallery controls, and motion effects.

## Technical details
- Store the uploaded flyer through the project asset flow and import its hosted URL.
- Preserve the current React routes for compatibility, while relabelling `/tours` as Accommodation.
- Replace unsupported placeholder links and invented business details with the information supplied.
- Verify the finished site on desktop and mobile, including links, menus, and visible text.
